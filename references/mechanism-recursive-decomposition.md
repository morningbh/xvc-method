# Mechanism Recursive Decomposition

Use this reference when the keystone uncertainty depends on a system mechanism rather than a simple fact.

## Five-layer decomposition

### Layer 1: outcome to explain
What outcome matters for the decision?

Examples:
- margin can remain high longer than expected
- users form a repeated habit
- supply shortage persists
- regulation changes the market structure
- a strategy produces durable advantage

### Layer 2: historical or base-rate mechanism
How has this outcome behaved historically, and why?

Extract:
- typical duration
- typical sequence
- common failure modes
- base rates
- incentives
- lags
- feedback loops

### Layer 3: current-cycle anomaly
What is different this time?

Classify the anomaly:
- demand strength
- demand durability
- supply response delay
- technology transition
- regulation
- financing constraint
- distribution change
- customer behavior
- market structure
- pricing mechanism

### Layer 4: duration mechanism
What determines how long the current condition can persist?

Look for:
- capacity lead time
- implementation lag
- customer switching cost
- inventory absorption
- adoption curve
- capital cycle
- learning curve
- institutional inertia
- contract structure
- bottleneck persistence

### Layer 5: falsification mechanism
What observable signal would show the mechanism is breaking?

Examples:
- price loosening
- lead-time compression
- churn rising
- usage frequency falling
- inventory building
- guidance changing
- customer behavior reversing
- competitor response accelerating
- regulatory enforcement shifting

## Output template

| layer | question | current hypothesis | evidence to check | kill signal | escalation trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Rival mechanism rule

Always include at least one rival mechanism.

A rival mechanism is not just a bearish or skeptical view. It is a different causal explanation for the same observations.

Examples:
- apparent durable demand is actually pull-forward demand
- apparent product-market fit is actually novelty
- apparent pricing power is actually temporary supply disruption
- apparent operational excellence is actually channel selection
- apparent technology edge is actually capital intensity

## Evidence discipline

Tag every mechanism link as:
- observed
- cited fact
- inferred
- assumed
- speculative

A mechanism with many speculative links should not be treated as strong even if the narrative is coherent.
